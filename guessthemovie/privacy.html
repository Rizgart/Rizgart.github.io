GuessTheMovie Duel

Privacy Policy · Last updated: 14 November 2026 · Support

About this policy

This policy describes how the GuessTheMovie Duel app handles information. GuessTheMovie Duel is a movie quiz where you challenge friends to duels through Game Center. The app is provided by Rizgar Teimouri (“we”).

Information we store on our server

  • Your Game Center player ID. When you sign in, the app sends a signature from Game Center that proves which player you are. We check it with Apple and store your Game Center player ID together with a random player number that we create. We do not receive your name, nickname, email address or friends. The names you see in the app come from Game Center on your device.
  • Your duels. For each duel we store the questions you got, your answers, when each question was shown and answered, your score and the state of the duel. We also keep a list of every question you have received, so that you never get the same question twice.
  • Reports. If you report a mistake in a question, we store the text you write together with the duel and the question.

Our server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and D1), which processes this information on our behalf. The database is stored in the European Union.

Technical information

When the app talks to our server, Cloudflare processes your IP address and technical details of the request to deliver it and to protect the service. Our server logs requests for a few days so that we can find and fix errors. We do not store IP addresses in our database.

How we use the information

We use the information only to run the game: to sign you in, to run your duels and work out the results, to make sure you never get the same question twice, to fix questions that are wrong, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell information, and we do not use your game data for advertising.

Advertising

The app shows a small banner from Google AdMob at the bottom of the screen, never while a question is shown.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app first asks for your consent through Google’s consent form. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • After that, iOS asks whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, Google may use your device’s advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you. If you do not, the ads are not based on tracking. You can change this under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.
  • Google may in any case process some information to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, such as your IP address (for approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data.
  • Your answers, scores and Game Center player ID are never sent with ad requests.

Read more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Legal bases

Under the GDPR we process your game data because it is needed to provide the game you use, technical logs because of our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working, and personalised ads and tracking only with your consent.

How long we keep information

We keep your game data for as long as you use the app. Technical logs are deleted automatically after a few days.

You can delete your data at any time under Settings → Delete my data. This removes your Game Center player ID, your question history and your reports from our server, and ends any duels in progress (your opponent wins by walkover). Finished duels stay so that your opponents’ results do not change, but they can no longer be linked to you. If you play again later, you start as a new player and may get questions you have seen before.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your information, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to get a copy of it. Contact us at the address below. You can also lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or the data protection authority where you live.

Children

GuessTheMovie Duel is not directed at children under 13.
