A movie quiz duel with your friends

Challenge a friend through Game Center and see who knows the most about movies. You both get the same questions in the same order, and you never get the same question twice, not even against a new opponent.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap + to challenge a friend and choose 5, 10 or 15 questions. The person you challenge plays first, then it’s your turn. Each question has a time limit. You see your own score when your turn is done, and the correct answers and your opponent’s score when you have both played.

Do I need Game Center?

Yes. The app uses Game Center for your opponents, challenges and turn notifications. Sign in under Settings → Game Center on your iPhone.

What happens if my opponent doesn’t play?

Each turn must be played within 72 hours. If it isn’t, the other player wins by walkover. A challenge that nobody accepts within 72 hours expires.

What does “Not enough new questions” mean?

You or your opponent have already had most of the questions for that length. Choose a shorter duel, or try again later when new questions have been added.

I found a mistake in a question.

Report it from the result screen with Report a problem with this question. We go through the reports and fix or remove wrong questions.

How do I change my ad choices?

Go to Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app. The option appears where it is required in your region.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Delete my data in the app. Your game history is deleted from our server, and you start over as a new player.
